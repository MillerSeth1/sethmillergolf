About Seth

I’m Seth Miller, an Army veteran and Staff Sergeant in the Arizona National Guard, living in Sahuarita, Arizona. I first enlisted in 2002.

On November 22, 2026, I joined the Veterans Golfers Association (VGA) to compete on the 2027 VGA Arizona Tour in the West Region. I’m starting the 2027 season as a new VGA competitor—no posted VGA rounds yet and no inflated claims—focused on showing up, posting official tournament scores, and building a steady season event by event.

Personal effort: This site and sponsorship ask are strictly personal—not affiliated with my employer or any outside business.
