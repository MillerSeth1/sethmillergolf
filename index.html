About

Golf is how I unwind. Picked the clubs back up in 2026 — chasing Nationals in 2027.

  • Army 2002–2013
  • Iraq 2006–07
  • Kuwait 2011–12
  • Now Arizona National Guard

I'm an Army veteran from Sahuarita, Arizona. I grew up in Kansas City and now call Southern Arizona home. I served from 2002 to 2013, including a 2003–04 mobilization to North Carolina backfilling active-duty units during Operation Enduring Freedom (OEF), and deployments to Iraq (2006–07) and Kuwait (2011–12). In Iraq, I was with an aviation battalion running daily missions all over the country, from hauling troops and gear to flying VIPs and senior leaders into Baghdad. I came back in 2019 to finish my career for my family, including a year on the Southwest border mission in Nogales, Arizona, and I now serve in the Arizona National Guard. I'm also a certified aircraft mechanic, a husband of 8 years, and dad to an 8-year-old son, plus two dogs, Bowie (15) and Abby (5). Golf is how I unwind — I picked the clubs back up in 2026 and set my sights on VGA Nationals in 2027, putting in the work and tracking every lesson, practice session, and round of the climb in my Season Log.

How it works

Businesses

Pick a $100, $250, or $500 season package and pay by card with the Polo, Hat, or Southwest tee button (choose "Business" at checkout), or email Seth for a Stripe invoice. Then send your logo. In-kind

Gear and discount codes are welcome. You'll get a thank-you post and a spot on the sponsor wall.

Individuals

Donate any amount with the Back my season button. To give $100 or more and get a thank-you gift, use the Polo, Hat, or Southwest tee buttons under Support and pick your size and color at checkout. Choose whether to show your name on the Supporters wall, or stay Anonymous. A numbered card I'll sign and mail myself comes with every gift.
