About Seth

I'm an Army veteran from Sahuarita, Arizona. I served from 2002 to 2013, with deployments to Iraq (2006–07) and Kuwait (2011–12). In Iraq, I was with an aviation battalion running daily missions all over the country, from hauling troops and gear to flying VIPs and senior leaders into Baghdad. I came back in 2019 to finish my career for my family, including a year on the Southwest border mission, and I now serve as a Staff Sergeant in the Arizona National Guard. I'm also a certified aircraft mechanic, a husband of 8 years, and dad to an 8-year-old son, plus two dogs, Bowie (15) and Abby (5). Golf is how I unwind, and in 2027 I'm taking it to the VGA tournament level.
